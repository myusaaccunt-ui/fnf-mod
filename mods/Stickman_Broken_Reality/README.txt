STICKMAN: BROKEN REALITY

Vanilla FNF 0.8.8 mod package.

Place this mod folder inside the game mod directory in a vanilla 0.8.8 build.

Required audio assets to add:
- mods/Stickman_Broken_Reality/songs/broken-reality/Inst.ogg
- mods/Stickman_Broken_Reality/songs/broken-reality/Voices.ogg

Graphics modes:
- LOW  = minimal shaders, minimal particles, reduced camera shake
- MEDIUM = balanced effects and particle counts
- HIGH = full glitch, distortion, bloom-like glow, and camera effects

This package intentionally does not use any Psych Engine-only structure or APIs.
